ShqipFol: the voice of Albanian in your pocket

Translate your voice in real time with ShqipFol, built for fast and natural communication.

The product

Breaking language barriers instantly

Whether you are travelling, in a business meeting or simply chatting with friends, ShqipFol understands your voice and responds with a natural, human-like voice.

ShqipFol is not just a translator. It is your personal live interpreter. Keep full control with Hold-to-Talk for more privacy and better performance in loud places.

Why ShqipFol

Simple. Fast. Professional.

Real-time translation

Speak naturally and hear the translation immediately, with no awkward pauses.

Human-like voices

Choose from natural voices (Bardha, Dielli, Genti) designed to sound authentic.

CarPlay support

Full CarPlay support and steering-wheel controls for safe translation while driving.

Auto language detection

Instantly detects whether you are speaking Albanian or a foreign language.
